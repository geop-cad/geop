The CAD app

Your parts

The CAD kernel is compiled to WebAssembly and runs in your browser. Parts you build, sketches you draw and files you load or save are processed only there. Saving downloads a file to your device; nothing is sent to us.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which features are used and which fail, the app sends the following events to PostHog (PostHog Inc.), hosted in PostHog's EU cloud in Frankfurt, Germany:

  • that the app was opened (page URL, referring page, browser and version, operating system, device type, screen and window size, browser language, time zone);
  • that a step was added, changed, removed or moved, and which kind of operation it is (e.g. “extrude”) — never its values, names or sketch;
  • when a step fails: its kind of operation and the kernel's error message, with every name removed;
  • which built-in example was opened, and that a file was saved or loaded (not its contents).

This runs in PostHog's cookieless mode: no cookie is set and nothing is stored on or read from your device. To count visitors, PostHog computes a hash of your IP address and browser identification together with a secret that changes every day; the IP address itself is not stored, and the hash cannot be linked across days. PostHog derives an approximate location (country, region) from the IP address before discarding it. We do not create user profiles and cannot identify you.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in finding and fixing failures and in understanding how the app is used. You can object at any time, with effect for the future, by switching statistics off under Privacy in the app; that choice is remembered in your browser's local storage (a single flag, used for nothing else). Statistics are also off if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. PostHog processes this data on our behalf under a data processing agreement. Statistics are kept for at most one year.

Bug reports

If you send a bug report from the app, we receive your description, the part you were working on (the program file), the page URL, your browser identification, the time and the last error message. They are stored in Cloudflare R2 and used only to reproduce and fix the bug, and deleted when no longer needed for that. To prevent abuse, your IP address is kept for up to 24 hours to limit how many reports can be sent per day. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in fixing reported bugs and protecting the service from abuse. Do not put personal information into the description if you do not want us to have it.
